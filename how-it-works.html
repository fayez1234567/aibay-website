How It Works

From finding a court to finding someone to play with, here’s how PadelBase is designed to work.

The intended player journey

  1. Find a court. Search for nearby clubs and filter by indoor or outdoor courts.
  2. Find a match. Choose a skill range, date and match type to look for a suitable game.
  3. Get on court. Booking, joining matches and paying your share are intended parts of the workflow. Public availability will be announced separately.
