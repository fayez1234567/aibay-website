About PadelBase

PadelBase is a court booking and matchmaking app for padel players and clubs.

Why PadelBase?

Organising a padel game means finding a court, finding players and agreeing on when to play. PadelBase is designed to bring those tasks together.

The app preview shows the experience from a player’s perspective. We are also exploring how clubs could manage availability and bookings in one place.
