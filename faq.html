Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about the app preview, planned features and registering your club.

What is PadelBase?

PadelBase is a padel court booking and player matchmaking app.

Can I download the app?

Verified App Store and Google Play links will appear when available.

Can I already book and pay for a court?

The preview shows the booking entry point. Live booking and payment availability are unconfirmed.

Will I be able to split the cost of a court?

Splitting court costs is a planned feature.

How can I enquire about registering a club?

Use the club registration form to prepare an enquiry.

How can I get in touch?

Go to the contact page to prepare your message.
