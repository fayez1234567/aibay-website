App Features

PadelBase brings court discovery and match search into one app. Explore the preview and see what’s planned.

Shown in the app preview

These screens show the app interface. Public availability is unconfirmed.

Discover and book padel courts

The preview shows club search, indoor and outdoor filters, and a View & Book entry point. Live availability and booking completion remain unverified.

Find matches

The preview shows skill-level, date and match-type filters, plus tabs for open matches and invites.

Player profiles and rankings

The preview shows player stats, booking and history tabs, skill divisions and rankings.

Planned features

Timing and availability will be confirmed as the product develops.

Create and join open matches

Organise a game and invite other players to fill available spots.

Find other padel players

Look for players by skill level and location.

Split court booking costs

Share the cost of a court among players.

Pay online

Complete a court payment through a secure checkout.

Manage upcoming bookings

See upcoming reservations and relevant booking details in one place.

Connect with the padel community

Find people to play with and stay in touch after a match.
